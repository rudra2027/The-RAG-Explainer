# Nimbus Insight - Product FAQ

## What is Nimbus Insight?

Nimbus Insight is our analytics platform. It connects to a customer's databases, builds dashboards automatically and sends alerts when a metric changes unexpectedly.

## Pricing

Nimbus Insight has three plans. The Starter plan costs 49 USD per month and includes 3 users and 5 dashboards. The Team plan costs 199 USD per month and includes 15 users and unlimited dashboards. The Enterprise plan has custom pricing and adds single sign-on, audit logs and a dedicated customer success manager.

Annual billing gives a 20% discount on the Starter and Team plans. Customers can start a free 14-day trial of the Team plan without a credit card.

## Release cycle

A new version of Nimbus Insight is released every two weeks on Tuesday. Release notes are published in the in-app "What's new" panel. Features marked as Beta can be switched on by workspace admins in Settings.

## Data connectors

Nimbus Insight connects to PostgreSQL, MySQL, Snowflake, BigQuery and Google Sheets. A connector for Microsoft SQL Server is on the roadmap for the next quarter.

## Customer support

Starter customers get email support with a response within 2 business days. Team customers get chat support with a response within 4 business hours. Enterprise customers get 24/7 phone support and a 1-hour response time for critical incidents.
