# Nimbus Analytics - Leave Policy

This policy explains the types of leave available to all permanent employees of Nimbus Analytics.

## Annual paid leave

Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their contracted hours. New joiners accrue leave from their first day at a rate of 2 days per month.

Up to 5 unused leave days can be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days must be used before 31 March, otherwise they expire. Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance for absences longer than 3 days.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from annual leave and is fully paid for up to 15 days per year. For absences longer than 3 consecutive days, employees must upload a doctor's note to the HR portal. Managers should be informed before 10:00 on the first day of sickness.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 26 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be taken in up to three separate blocks within the first year after the birth or adoption.

Employees returning from parental leave may work a reduced 4-day week at 80% pay for up to 6 months, after which they return to their previous schedule.

## Public holidays

Nimbus Analytics observes all national public holidays. If a public holiday falls on a weekend, employees receive the following Monday off instead.

## Unpaid leave and sabbaticals

After 3 years of service, employees may request an unpaid sabbatical of up to 3 months. Sabbatical requests need approval from both the line manager and the HR business partner and should be submitted at least 3 months before the start date.
