# Nimbus Analytics - Travel and Expense Policy

## Submitting expenses

All business expenses must be submitted in the Expensa app within 30 days of the purchase. Every expense needs a photo of the itemised receipt. Expenses submitted after 30 days require written approval from the finance director.

Approved expenses are reimbursed with the next monthly salary payment, provided they are submitted before the 20th of the month.

## Travel

Economy class is the standard for all flights under 6 hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights longer than 6 hours. Business class requires approval from a C-level executive.

Train travel is preferred over flying for journeys under 4 hours. Trips must be booked through the TravelDesk portal so the company can locate employees in an emergency.

## Hotels and meals

The hotel budget is 180 EUR per night in major cities and 120 EUR per night elsewhere. The daily meal allowance during business travel is 60 EUR. Alcohol is not reimbursed, except at client dinners approved in advance by a director.

## Equipment and home office

Each employee may claim up to 400 EUR once every 2 years for home-office equipment such as a chair, desk or monitor. Laptops and phones are provided by IT and cannot be claimed as expenses.

## Corporate cards

Employees who travel more than 4 times per year can request a corporate credit card. Personal purchases on a corporate card are not allowed and must be repaid within 7 days.
