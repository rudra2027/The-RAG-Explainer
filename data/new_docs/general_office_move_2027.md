# Announcement - New Head Office

Nimbus Analytics is moving its head office to the Riverside Tower, 14th floor, on Monday 1 March 2027.

## What changes for employees

The new office has 220 desks, 12 meeting rooms and a rooftop terrace. Desks are booked through the DeskFinder app; desks can be reserved up to 2 weeks ahead.

Bicycle parking for 80 bikes is available in the basement, and employees get a free monthly public-transport pass instead of a car parking space.

## Moving week

The old office closes on Friday 26 February 2027. Everyone works from home during moving week. Each employee receives one moving crate on 15 February; crates must be labelled with your name and team.

Questions about the move go to the facilities team at move@nimbus-analytics.example.
